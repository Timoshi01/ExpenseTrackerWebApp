📘 Visual step‑by‑step guide

Real screenshots + instructions – everything you need to master PennyPilot.
0

🌐 Open web app & install on phone

Click the link: pennypilotexpensetracker1.netlify.app
When you open it, you’ll see the dashboard: total spent, monthly progress, budget left, and bottom navigation.
💡 Install as app (Android/iOS): Chrome → three dots ⋮ → “Add to Home Screen” / “Install App”. Works offline afterwards.
Home dashboard: total spent, monthly budget progress, budget left card
1

🔐 Sign in with Google (cloud sync)

Go to Settings (bottom navigation) → tap "Sign in with Google". Choose your Gmail account.
✅ After login: your expenses are automatically backed up to cloud. Change device? just login again → all data returns.
Settings panel: sign in with Google, dark mode, updates & data management
🔁 After login, “Synced” badge appears — your data is safe.
2

💰 Set your budget goal (monthly/weekly/daily)

Navigate to the Budget tab → choose “Monthly budget” (or weekly/daily) → enter amount (e.g., ₱1000) → click “Set Goal & Go Home”.
You’ll see real‑time budget progress on the dashboard. Use “Reset Budget” to start a fresh cycle (old expenses stay in history).
Budget view: set monthly/weekly goal, reset button & Budget vs Actual chart
3

➕ Add an expense (custom category!)

Tap ➕ Add (center button).
• Category: you can type anything — “Plete bus”, “Coffee”, “School supplies”.
• Amount: positive number only (no negative pls 😅).
• Date: auto filled (you can change).
• Note: optional but helpful.
Click “Add Expense” → success notification → new expense appears in Home & History.
Add expense: custom category “Plete bus”, amount ₱80, date auto.
4

📅 History, Heatmap & Smart Filters

Activity Heatmap (last 30 days) shows your consistency — greener = more active days.
• Filter by date range → click filter icon, choose period (day/week/month).
• Save File / Export: after filtering, tap “Save file” → download a TXT with all expenses inside that period. Perfect for sending proof to parents or personal records.
Every expense appears in history with amount, category, date & note.
History: heatmap (last 30 days), filter control & save file button
5

📆 Calendar: daily overview

Tap the Calendar tab → see month view. Tap any day → shows day total & list of expenses for that exact date.
Perfect for tracking “how much did I spend last Tuesday?”
Calendar: pick a date, see day total & expenses for that day.
